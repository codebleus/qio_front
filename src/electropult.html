typography

The combination of IBM Plex Sans and DrukCyr creates a balanced typographic system that merges functionality with expression. Together, they reflect the dual nature of the project — technological precision and industrial strength. The contrast between a clean, neutral sans-serif and a bold, condensed display typeface gives the interface both clarity and character.

IBM Plex Sans

Chosen as the primary text font, IBM Plex Sans provides excellent legibility and a modern, professional tone. Its open forms and well-balanced proportions ensure smooth reading across digital environments. The typeface conveys transparency, innovation, and technical confidence — perfectly aligned with the brand’s forward-looking identity.

DrukCyr

Used for headlines and key accents, DrukCyr adds a sense of power and industrial energy. Its condensed, heavy structure gives visual weight and focus, emphasizing the brand’s authority and reliability. DrukCyr’s strong geometry complements IBM Plex Sans, reinforcing the project’s overall aesthetic of precision and stability.
